About Shammy's Clothing

Shammy's Clothing is built on a simple belief: great clothes shouldn't shout. They should fit quietly into your life, season after season, year after year.

Our story

Shammy's started in Melbourne with one rule: only stock what we’d actually wear. We carry brands we respect — NN.07, Fred Perry, Levi’s, Sebago, Anderson’s, Larriet, Moscot, Casio, Sanders, Kamo International, Fracap and By Oli Oli — and present them with the same care we’d want as customers.

Everything here is chosen for longevity, not for the next trend. Timeless cuts, honest materials, no pretense.
